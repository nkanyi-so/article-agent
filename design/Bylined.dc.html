How Acme rebuilt its deploy pipeline

From a 32-minute standup with Jane Doe, VP Engineering · 7 grounded sources

When Jane Doe joined Acme in 2023, deploys were a weekly event the whole team scheduled around.[1] Within eighteen months her group moved the company to continuous delivery, shipping to production more than forty times a day.[2]

The rebuild started with the test suite. By parallelizing integration tests across ephemeral environments, the team cut median CI time from 41 minutes to under nine.[3] That single change, Doe noted in the standup, “unblocked everything downstream.”

The investment paid off in reliability, too: the platform team reports it cut incidents by roughly 40% over the same period.⚑ no source
