Notes from the allotment society's spring meeting, written up afterwards from memory.

The meeting opened at seven in the village hall with about thirty members present. The
treasurer reported that the society holds a little over two thousand pounds, most of it
set aside for the new water trough. There was a long discussion about the hedge on the
north side, which several members said had not been cut for two years and was shading
the first row of plots. It was agreed to ask for quotes from two contractors before the
next meeting. The secretary reminded everyone that rents are due by the end of the month
and that the waiting list now stands at eleven names. A member asked whether bonfires
would be allowed again this autumn and was told the rule has not changed. The meeting
closed at half past eight with thanks to those who brought the tea.
