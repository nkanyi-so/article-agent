Calum Reddy: This is the Friday transport hour. With me are Ione Farraday, operations manager at Marrowbeck Ferries, and Bram Oxley, the harbour master at Marrowbeck. Thank you both.
Ione Farraday: Good to be here.
Bram Oxley: Morning.
Calum Reddy: Ione, start with the basics. What does the service normally run?
Ione Farraday: Six sailings a day between Marrowbeck and Tarn Island, with two vessels, the Skerry Lass and the Eilean.
Calum Reddy: And October was a bad month.
Ione Farraday: It was. We cancelled 14 sailings in October.
Calum Reddy: Why?
Ione Farraday: Weather. We had gusts over 40 knots on several days, and we do not sail in that.
Bram Oxley: I have to disagree with that. My log shows weather closed the harbour on two days. That accounts for 5 of the 14 sailings.
Calum Reddy: And the other 9?
Bram Oxley: The other 9 were cancelled on days when the harbour was open and other boats were moving. My understanding is the Eilean had no master.
Ione Farraday: The Eilean's master was off sick for part of the month, that is true.
Calum Reddy: So was it weather or was it crew?
Ione Farraday: I would still say weather was the main cause. Conditions outside the harbour can be worse than inside it.
Bram Oxley: I would say crew was the main cause. Nine is more than five.
Calum Reddy: You are not going to agree on that.
Ione Farraday: Probably not today.
Calum Reddy: What happened to the passengers?
Ione Farraday: We refunded 312 passengers in October. Everyone who asked got their money back within a week.
Calum Reddy: Is the crew problem fixed?
Ione Farraday: We have hired a second master for the Eilean. She starts on the first of December.
Bram Oxley: That will help. A boat with one master is one cold away from staying tied up.
Calum Reddy: Bram, anything the harbour is changing?
Bram Oxley: We are putting a wind gauge on the outer wall, so that both of us are reading the same number. It goes in next month.
Ione Farraday: I welcome that. It would have saved us this argument.
Calum Reddy: Ione Farraday, Bram Oxley, thank you.
